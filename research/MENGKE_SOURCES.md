# 《奇妙萌可之闪耀流星》24 只萌可：来源与核对记录 (researched 2026-10-21)

## 结论
The 24 names are consistent across all sources listed below. Two things are not clean, so read before using the list:
1. **流浪萌可 and 王子萌可 are the same character.** He first appears as 流浪萌可 (ep. 7) and turns back into 王子萌可 in ep. 23–24 (Baidu Baike 王子萌可 entry). The fandom wiki counts "23 Star Teeniepings" plus Heartsping. The Chinese list of "24 Mengke" counts both forms separately. The firmware shows both because each has its own name and official render.
2. **爱心萌可 is not new this season.** She is a carry-over character (Star Heartsping costume). 银光/星光/月光/王子 are royal Mengke (皇室萌可), and 极光 is the legend Mengke (传奇萌可).

## Discrepancies found between sources
- Voice cast: Baidu lists 银光 = 苏婉 and 星光 = 王晓彤. 萌娘百科 lists them the other way round. This does not affect the firmware.
- 星光 attribute: 百度 says 心愿, maigoo says 希望. The firmware uses 心愿.
- Season numbering: Chinese sources call this season 6 (第6季). The fandom wiki calls it "season 5".
